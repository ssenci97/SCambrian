Help
  • Checklists: click toggles · double-click isolates · ⓘ inspects · ▲ ▼ draws in front / behind · swatch repicks the colour (shift-click resets)
  • Ticks of several series, samples, features and the lasso combine (AND); each active filter is a pill above the view, × removes it
  • Features: tick terms to keep the proteins carrying them (OR, or AND with the switch); several features AND
  • 2D: lasso filters points · double-click clears
  • Click a point, a bar, a flux node or a network node to inspect it; bars and flux nodes also isolate their cluster
  • Enrichment: clusters of any series against uniprot_family, GO and every other feature (hypergeometric test, BH q); Series summary scores every series against the features; click a cell to inspect or drill down
  • Network: click a node keeps its proteins · shift-click adds it to the term filter
  • ⚙ Appearance: every font, palette colour, special colour and category colour; settings persist in this browser and export to JSON
